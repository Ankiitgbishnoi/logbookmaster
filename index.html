How the calculator works

Understand what these figures do and do not mean.

Daily work and rest totals are derived from saved activity intervals. Seven-day and fourteen-day summaries use elapsed time immediately before the current moment. These are descriptive summaries and do not replace legal advice.

The app does not yet automatically determine local-area versus interstate diary obligations, establish legally qualifying rest breaks, apply every hours-option variation, validate accreditations, or certify compliance with NHVR rules.

Important: Logbook Master Personal Edition is an independent prototype, not an NHVR product and not an approved Electronic Work Diary. Calculations are preliminary summaries only and should not be relied upon for legal compliance.
