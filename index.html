Increase safety on the road — plan your hours, rest well.

Logbook Master helps heavy vehicle drivers stay compliant and safe by making work/rest planning simple and visible. Use the live drive controls to track shifts and export a printable logbook when you finish.

Quick safety checklist

  • Plan breaks: stop every 2 hours for short rest.
  • Check vehicle pre-trip: lights, brakes, tyres.
  • Don't drive fatigued — record rest accurately.
  • Keep mobile devices stowed; use hands-free for calls.
