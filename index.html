How the calculator works

Understand what these figures do and do not mean.

Daily work and rest totals are derived from saved activity intervals. Seven-day and fourteen-day summaries use elapsed time immediately before the current moment. These are descriptive summaries, not a complete implementation of statutory counting rules.

The app does not yet automatically determine local-area versus interstate diary obligations, establish legally qualifying rest breaks, apply every hours-option variation, validate accreditation conditions, or verify permits and vehicle access.

Vehicle configurations are descriptive labels. Selecting a B-double, road train, triple or quad combination does not establish that it is permitted on a particular road.

Important: Logbook Master Personal Edition is an independent prototype, not an NHVR product and not an approved Electronic Work Diary. Calculations are preliminary summaries only. Do not rely on this prototype as your sole record where legislation requires a work diary or other record. Verify your obligations using current official guidance and your applicable accreditation conditions. Never drive while fatigued or unfit to drive.
